STEM Topic

PID Control in Drone Flight

Discover how a closed-loop PID controller translates position error into thrust and tilt commands so a drone can achieve stable takeoff, hover, movement, and precise positioning.

STEM Activity

Tune the PID Controller and Fly Your Drone

Adjust three controller gains and pilot manually with WASD to observe real flight dynamics:

  • Kp — Proportional Gain
  • Ki — Integral Gain
  • Kd — Derivative Gain
Responsiveness Overshoot Oscillation Steady-State Error Stability Settling Time
Learning Objectives

What You Will Master

  1. Explain the basic roles of P, I and D.
  2. Observe how PID gains affect drone movement.
  3. Identify overshoot, oscillation and slow response.
  4. Improve stability through iterative tuning.
  5. Apply feedback control to complete flight missions.
How It Works

Three Steps to Flight Mastery

  1. 1
    Learn the basics of PID control. Explore gravity, thrust, and each PID term in Tutorial Village.
  2. 2
    Tune Kp, Ki and Kd. Experiment with gain sliders and watch real physical responses.
  3. 3
    Fly with WASD and complete missions. Guide your drone through hover, wind, rings, and precision landings.
