          About the Course

          Drone Lab — PID Flight Challenge

          STEM Topic

          Proportional–Integral–Derivative (PID) Control

          STEM Activity

          Tuning a PID controller to control a drone.

          Branches of STEM

          • Technology: Drone flight control systems.
          • Engineering (Primary Focus): PID controller design, tuning and optimisation.

          Game Description

          Drone Lab — PID Flight Challenge is an interactive STEM game where players tune Kp, Ki and Kd, then use WASD to fly a simulated drone through different challenges. Poor tuning can cause overshoot, oscillation or slow responses. Players improve their settings through experimentation and earn up to three stars.

          Learning Objectives

          1. Explain how Kp, Ki and Kd affect drone stability and responsiveness.
          2. Adjust PID parameters to improve flight control.
          3. Identify overshoot, oscillation and slow response.
          4. Apply experimentation and feedback to solve flight-control problems.
